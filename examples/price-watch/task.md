Open http://localhost:8765/ with the browser and read the name, price and stock of every
product in the catalog (product filter: {product}). Compare the prices with the previous
values in http://localhost:8765/previous.json. If any price changed or any product went out of
stock, post a short alert to #alerts with the alerts tool, listing the changed products and
their old and new prices; otherwise do not post anything. Use only the browser and alerts tools.
